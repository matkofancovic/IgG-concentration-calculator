===========================================================================
  GlycanAge plate run  -  how to install it and how the sharing works
===========================================================================

WHAT IT IS

  One program for a GlycanAge plate's paperwork.  You give it two files -
  the Pippeting List and the NanoDrop .txt - and it produces:

    - the concentration workbook (Well / Sample ID / conc., colour coded,
      with the DBS and standard averages)
    - the IgG isolation worksheet, filled in, with the real coloured plate
      layout printed on it
    - the deglycosylation and clean-up worksheets, filled in


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  INSTALLING IT  -  once per PC, takes a minute
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  There is nothing to install.  You make a shortcut to a file on the
  Glikobiologija share and that is it.

  1.  Open this folder in Explorer:

        \\10.70.119.100\Glikobiologija\Python programs\IgG Concentration Builder

  2.  Right-click on

        Run IgG Concentration Builder.bat

      and choose  "Send to"  ->  "Desktop (create shortcut)".

  3.  Double-click that shortcut on your desktop.  That is the program.

  IMPORTANT:  make the shortcut to the .bat file, NOT to the .exe.
  The .bat is what keeps you up to date - it checks the share each time you
  start it and copies the newest version to your own PC before running it.
  If you shortcut the .exe you will be stuck on whatever version you copied.

  THE FIRST TIME ONLY, Windows may show a blue box saying
  "Windows protected your PC".  That is because the program is not signed,
  not because anything is wrong.  Click  "More info"  ->  "Run anyway".
  It will not ask again on that PC.

  If your antivirus blocks it instead, send the folder path to IT and ask
  them to allow it.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW THE SHARING WORKS  -  "if I do something, how do the others see it?"
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Everything the program remembers is kept in two small files in that same
  share folder, NOT on your PC:

        consumables.json     the LOT numbers and the solution library
        runs.json            which plate got how far

  So there is nothing to send anyone.  You record something, they open the
  program, they see it.  Concretely:

  *  YOU OPEN A NEW wwPTFE PLATE and type its LOT number.
     The next person to open the program is offered that LOT as the current
     one, already filled in.  The old LOTs stay in the drop-down.

  *  YOU MAKE UP A SOLUTION and press "+" next to it (just the date).
     Everyone is offered that preparation from the drop-down.  When the
     bottle is used up, press "-" and it comes off the list for everybody.
     No volumes and no stock levels - the bottle in the fridge is the
     authority on that.

  *  YOU USE A PROTEIN G PLATE.
     The program counts it.  Nobody types "how many times has this plate
     been used" any more - it knows, across all of you, because the count
     is on the share.

  *  YOU ENTER THE WORKSHEET NUMBERS for a plate on day 1.
     On day 2, on any PC, click that plate in the list on the left and
     everything you typed comes back.  It does not have to be the same
     person or the same computer.

  WHEN DOES IT UPDATE?
     The list refreshes when you open the program, and after you build
     something.  There is a "Refresh" button under the solution library if
     you want to pull in what somebody just did while you had it open.

  IF TWO OF YOU SAVE AT THE SAME MOMENT
     Nothing breaks.  Each save re-reads the file and merges into it, so
     the worst that can happen is one edit is lost, never a broken file.

  IF THE SHARE IS DOWN
     The program still works.  It keeps its notes on your own PC for the
     time being and says so at the bottom of the window.  Those notes stay
     on that PC though - they do not get pushed to the share afterwards.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  USING IT  -  a normal plate
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  THE DATES
     It is a three day job.  Put the DAY 1 date in and days 2 and 3 fill
     themselves in on the next working days - start on a Friday and you get
     Monday and Tuesday, not the weekend.

     Click a date box (or the little calendar button beside it) and a
     calendar opens on the date that is already there, so you can just click
     the day you want.  "Today" jumps to today.  Type it by hand if you
     prefer - either works.

  DAY 1
   1.  "New plate..." (left) and pick the plate's Pippeting List.
   2.  Type the first worksheet number, press "Fill down" - the rest follow
       in order.  Same for the storage numbers.
   3.  Check the filter plate LOT and the Protein G plate number that are
       offered.  CHECK THEM AGAINST THE LABEL IN FRONT OF YOU - the program
       offers what was used last, which is a convenience, not proof.
   4.  Press "IgG isolation worksheet", then "Print".
   5.  Go and do the isolation.

  DAY 2
   6.  Open the program, click the plate in the list on the left.
       Everything from day 1 comes back.
   7.  Put the NanoDrop .txt in.
   8.  Press "Workbook + deglyco + clean up".

  WHERE THE FILES GO
     Everything is saved to your own Desktop, in

         Desktop\GlycanAge plate runs\<plate>
     Nothing is ever written onto the Glikobiologija share.

  THE BLANK WORKSHEETS
     The first time you build something the program asks you to point at the
     folder holding the blank worksheet PDFs (GBL-WS-029, 030, 031, 002).
     It remembers that afterwards.  It does not go looking on the share by
     itself.

  PRINTING
     Printed worksheets ask the printer for double-sided (flipped on the long
     edge).  Most readers honour it; if yours prints single-sided, tick
     double-sided in the print dialog once and it will stick.

  ANY TIME
   *  "Concentration workbook only" builds just the spreadsheet, without
      touching the worksheets.
   *  "Storage worksheets (x3)" fills one GBL-WS-002 per storage number -
      IgG eluate, dry IgG, APTS glycans - each with the coloured sample
      grid.  The freezer letter and drawer number are left blank for you to
      write in at the freezer.
   *  The four steps across the top - Plate, Numbers, Materials, Build -
      show one panel at a time.  Click along them; nothing is hidden.
   *  Nothing is ever locked.  Every button can be pressed again, in any
      order, as many times as you like.  Building something twice does not
      double-count the solutions or the Protein G plate.

  ANYTHING YOU LEAVE EMPTY PRINTS EMPTY, so you can always fill a field in
  by hand instead.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  THE PLATE LIST DOWN THE LEFT
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

        (tick)   all three worksheets built
        (half)   part way through
        (dot)    nothing built yet

  "Remove" only makes the program forget a plate.  It never deletes any
  worksheet or workbook you have already saved.


---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  WHO TO ASK
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Something looks wrong on a printed worksheet, or a number is not what you
  expect:  check the Report box at the bottom of the window first.  It says
  what it filled in and, more usefully, what it could NOT find.

  The amounts of solution per plate are worked out from the worksheets
  themselves.  Press "Where does (calc) come from?" and it prints the sum
  for every solution into the Report so you can check it against the SOP.
